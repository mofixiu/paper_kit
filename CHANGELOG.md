## 0.1.0

First release.

- `PaperTheme`, `PaperThemeData` and `PaperPalette`: cream and espresso paper, with
  `toMaterialTheme()` for Material widgets and a `fontBuilder` hook for font packages.
- `PaperInkReveal`: theme changes spread over the screen like a drop of ink.
- `PaperBackground` with a procedural paper grain (no image assets).
- `PaperHeading`, `PaperThemeToggle`, `PaperStampButton` and `PaperStampImpression`,
  `PaperCheckbox`, `PaperTextField`, `PaperIndexTabs`, `PaperTearToConfirm`,
  `PaperReceipt`, `PaperPlaneButton`, `PaperPeel`, `PaperZine`, `PaperNote` and
  `PaperRuler`.
- `PaperPhoneFrame`, `PaperFingertip`, `PaperDemoController` and `PaperDemoScript` for
  showing an app in an iPhone with a scripted fingertip that taps the real widgets.
- `PaperFadeUpRoute` and `PaperFadeUpTransition`.
